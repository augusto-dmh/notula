Notula browser extension: privacy policy

Applies to the Notula extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge, version 1. Last updated 2026-10-20.

The Notula extension tells the Notula app on your computer what is playing in your browser, so your notes link to the video or lesson you’re watching. It has no server. Nothing it reads goes to Notula’s developers or to anyone else.

What the extension reads

On YouTube, on sites you turn on, and on a tab you link from the extension’s button:

  • the playing video’s address, title, current time, duration, speed and play state;
  • whether an ad is showing;
  • the course, section and lesson shown on the page, and on YouTube the playlist and channel;
  • video captions or a transcript the page has already loaded, or one you ask it to read.

On YouTube, once you accept the explanation in the extension, it also watches the caption files the YouTube player downloads, so it can pass them on without asking YouTube again. It doesn’t change those files or what the page shows.

It never reads cookies, passwords, form fields, comments, your account name, other tabs or your browsing history.

Where it goes

Only to the Notula app on the same computer, through the browser’s native messaging. The extension sends nothing over the internet. Notula keeps these details only for the videos and lessons you take notes on.

When

Only while a video plays on YouTube or on a site you turned on, or when you press Mark this moment (Alt+Shift+M). Before you first accept the explanation in the extension, it sends nothing.

The course, section, lesson, playlist and channel are sent only while Course pages is on in Notula ▸ Settings ▸ Sources. Captions are sent only while Captions from the player is on there.

Private windows

The extension runs in private (incognito or InPrivate) windows only if you allow it there in your browser. Even then, nothing is sent from a private window unless you also turn on its Allow in private windows switch and turn on private windows in Notula. All three are off by default.

Network requests

The extension makes one kind of request of its own: when Notula asks for a video’s captions and the YouTube player hasn’t loaded them, it asks YouTube for that caption track, the same way the player does. It never makes this request when Notula is in local-only mode.

Limited Use

The use of information received from this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Turn a site off in the extension’s settings, or remove its access in your browser.
  • Turn off the browser extension in Notula ▸ Settings ▸ Sources, or uninstall the extension.
  • Delete notes in Notula to delete what it kept about their videos.
